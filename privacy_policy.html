Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 7, 2026

At NYC Slice of Life (nycsliceoflife.com), we value your privacy. This page explains what information may be collected when you visit our site and how it may be used.

1. What We Collect

We do not ask you for personal information. There are no accounts, logins, or signup forms on this site.

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4, a service provided by Google LLC, to understand how visitors use the site — which pages are viewed, and which features are used. This helps us decide what to build next.

Google Analytics does not run unless you choose “Accept.” When you first visit, a banner asks whether you consent. If you decline, ignore the banner, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, no analytics are loaded and no analytics cookies are set.

If you do consent, Google Analytics sets cookies on your device to recognise repeat visits, and collects: pages viewed, approximate location derived from your IP address, browser and device type, and which on-site features you interact with. Your IP address is used by Google to estimate general location and is not stored by us.

We do not send Google any information you type into the site. When you use the search box, we record only how long your search was and how many results it returned — never the text itself.

Analytics data is retained for 2 months, after which Google deletes it. We have disabled Google Signals, demographic and interest reporting, and all advertising and remarketing features.

2. Your Choices

You can change your mind at any time using the “Cookie settings” link in the footer of every page, or the button below. Withdrawing is exactly as easy as granting.

If you withdraw consent, we stop sending data to Google Analytics and delete the analytics cookies this site set. Data already collected before you withdrew is removed by Google at the end of the 2-month retention period.

We honour the Global Privacy Control (GPC) and Do Not Track browser signals. If your browser sends either, we treat that as a refusal and never show you the consent banner.

3. Third-Party Advertising

This site does not display ads. We do not sell or share your information with anyone, and we have turned off Google’s advertising and remarketing features.

4. Children’s Privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. We do not ask for anyone’s age and we do not use any feature that attempts to infer it.

5. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. All changes will be reflected on this page.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at NYCSliceofLife@gmail.com.
